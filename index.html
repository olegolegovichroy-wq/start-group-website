What we do

Six services, one point of contact. We stay on the Chinese side of the table on your behalf, from the first search to the last shipment.

  • Supplier sourcing

    We find and qualify manufacturers in China, India and Southeast Asia for your exact specification, then shortlist only those we would work with ourselves.

  • Negotiation and representation

    We negotiate on your behalf in Chinese, on site, so prices, terms and deadlines are agreed the way they will actually be executed.

  • Quality control

    Factory audits, in-process and pre-shipment inspections, documentation review. You see what leaves the factory before it ships.

  • Contract and legal support

    Contracts with Chinese companies that hold up in practice: drafting, review and enforcement support together with local counsel.

  • China business consulting

    Practical advice on how business is actually done in China: partners, payments, customs, culture and risk.

  • Western market entry for Chinese companies

    For Chinese manufacturers: positioning, partner search and representation in European markets.

How we work

Four steps. You know at every stage what has been done, what it cost and what comes next.

  1. Brief

    You describe the task. We reply within one business day with questions and a plan.

  2. Search and verify

    We find candidates, check them on the ground and send you a shortlist with facts, not promises.

  3. Negotiate and contract

    We agree terms in your interest and put them into a contract that works in China.

  4. Control and deliver

    We inspect, report and stay with the deal until goods arrive and partners pay.
